Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026.

clip-pipeline runs locally on its owner's computer. When the owner connects a social account, the tool stores the access tokens and basic profile information (account name, avatar, statistics) locally to publish videos and show their status. This data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data is collected from anyone other than the owner. Revoking access in the platform's settings, or deleting the local installation, removes it.
